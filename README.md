Practical translation: if you're trying GreenCloud for the first time and want the refund safety net, buy a regular plan with PayPal or a card, not a coded flash deal with Bitcoin. **CPU is shared, with a 30% average rule.** Except for VDS plans, KVM cores are shared, and the terms say average usage should stay under 30% of a core, with bursts to 100% allowed up to 10 minutes per 24 hours.
